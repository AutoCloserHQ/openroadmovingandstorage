A move is the start of something.

A new home. A new city. A different pace. Open Road Moving & Storage is built around helping you start that next chapter with a clear conversation about the move ahead.

A national outlook, grounded in your route.

Our long-distance focus includes pickup coverage centered on Texas, the Midwest and Northeast. We review your actual origin, destination, inventory and dates before confirming what is available.

Clarity before commitment.

We want you to understand the services being discussed, the company handling your move and the terms in your written estimate. Ask questions about timing, packing, access, payments and storage before booking.

The company behind the name.

Open Road Moving & Storage is operated by TX Moving and Storage LLC. The company identifiers supplied for this service are USDOT 3266915 and MC 1030727. Your written moving documents identify the contracting company and the services and terms that apply to your shipment.
