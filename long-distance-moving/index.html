A thoughtful start to a bigger move.

Crossing state lines takes more than a destination ZIP code. Your inventory, building access, packing needs and timing shape the plan. Start by sharing the move you actually need, so those details can be reviewed before you book.

What to have ready

  • Pickup and delivery addresses or ZIP codes, plus any flexibility in dates.
  • A room-by-room inventory, including large or unusually fragile items.
  • Stairs, elevators, parking restrictions and the distance from the truck to your door.
  • Any packing, unpacking or storage help you want included.

Timing deserves its own conversation.

Ask whether pickup and delivery are scheduled for specific dates or a delivery window. Discuss access hours, travel plans and what happens if your destination is not ready. A requested date is not a guarantee unless your written agreement says so.

Know the company and the terms.

Your written moving estimate and agreement should identify the company handling your move, the services included, the applicable charges and the contact for shipment updates. Review these before paying or signing.
